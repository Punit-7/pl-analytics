# Annotation guidelines

Rules for labelling players, clubs and stadiums in Wikipedia club season paragraphs. One annotator;
labelled in Label Studio.

## Labels

| Label | Label these | Do not label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLAYER | Footballers' names: full names (`Bukayo Saka`), surnames (`Saka`) and single names (`Rodri`), for any club or country | Managers, coaches, referees, owners, pundits |
| TEAM | Club names and established short names: `Arsenal`, `Manchester City`, `Spurs`, `City` or `United` when they clearly mean the club, nicknames such as `Gunners` | National teams, competitions (`Premier League`, `FA Cup`), generic words (`the hosts`, `the visitors`) |
| VENUE | Stadium names: `Anfield`, `Emirates Stadium`, `Old Trafford`, `Wembley` | Cities and areas (`Manchester`, `north London`) |

## Boundary rules

- Label the name only: no leading `the`, no trailing punctuation, no possessive `'s`. In
  `Saka's goal`, label `Saka`.
- Label a full name as one span, not first name and surname separately.
- Label every mention, including repeats in the same paragraph.
- When you genuinely cannot decide, leave it unlabelled and record the case in the notes below.

## Notes on decided cases

Each note applies a rule above to a case that came up while labelling.

- 2026-10-07 — **Former players who are now managers.** Label by the role in the paragraph. `Arteta`
  is PLAYER in the 2015/16 Arsenal article and unlabelled from 2019/20, where he is the manager. The
  same applies to coaching staff named in an appointment (`Marco Silva`, `João Pedro Sousa`).
- 2026-10-07 — **One span per mention, one label per span.** Never put a second label on the same
  text or a span inside another span. `Romelu Lukaku` is one PLAYER span; do not also label
  `Lukaku` inside it. `Van Dijk` is one span, not `Van Dijk` plus `Dijk`.
- 2026-10-07 — **Longest name wins.** Label `Brighton and Hove Albion`, `West Ham United`, `Stoke
  City` and `Borussia Dortmund` as whole spans, not the short form inside them.
- 2026-10-07 — **Venue names that end in `'s`.** The `'s` is part of the name: `St Mary's`,
  `St James' Park`. The no-possessive rule applies to players and clubs only.
- 2026-10-07 — **A player's name is never TEAM.** In `Rice's goal` or `Arteta's side`, the name is a
  person. Label the player (or nothing, for a manager), not TEAM.
- 2026-10-07 — **Clubs outside the Premier League.** Foreign and lower-league clubs are TEAM:
  `Valencia`, `Paris Saint-Germain`, `Leyton Orient`, `AFC Wimbledon`. So are their grounds as VENUE
  (`Parc des Princes`).
- 2026-10-07 — **Short names and nicknames.** `United`, `City`, `Palace`, `Reds`, `Blues` and
  `Saints` are TEAM every time they mean the club, including repeats later in the paragraph. They
  are not labelled inside a competition or place name.
- 2026-10-07 — **Words that are also names.** `March` is PLAYER only when it means Solly March, not
  the month.
- 2026-10-07 — **Paragraphs with no entities.** Table notes, kit descriptions and fixture-list
  announcements are submitted with no labels.
